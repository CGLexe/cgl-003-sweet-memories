## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory intimacy and understated luxury of an artisanal floral atelier rooted in Oaxaca de Juárez. It marries European high-fashion editorial restraint with the natural vibrancy, warmth, and romanticism of luxury botanical design. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Quiet Luxury & Spatial Generosity**: Expansive, uncluttered white space allowing bespoke arrangements, lush peonies, and organic textures to take center stage.
- **Editorial Poise**: High-contrast, classic editorial serif typography paired with crisp, tracking-optimized humanist sans-serif for functional reading.
- **Warm Tactile Nuances**: Whispers of warm stone, paper textures, 1px hairline borders, and translucent rose and botanical washes that replace heavy drop shadows.
- **Effortless Commerce**: Frictionless, button-minimal interfaces optimized for tactile mobile discovery and immediate, bespoke concierge service via WhatsApp.

## Colors

The color palette reflects romantic petals, verdant foliage, and sunlit canopies, grounded by deep espresso typography and warm stone canvas backdrops:

- **Primary (`#C94C6D`)**: Deep Petal Rose. Used for primary CTAs, key accents, active states, and editorial highlights. Supported by `#E8728F` (blush accent) and `#FDF2F4` (delicate rose mist tint).
- **Secondary (`#5A7859`)**: Botanical Olive / Sage. Evoking freshly cut stems, eucalyptus, and garden greenery. Used for botanical badges, tags, success states, and subtle organic iconography. Supported by `#8FA88E`.
- **Tertiary (`#DFB976`)**: Warm Champagne Gold. An artisanal metallic accent reserved for bespoke services, curated bridal labels, and subtle micro-highlights.
- **Neutrals**:
  - Deep Ground (`#1E1B18`): Charcoal espresso for primary headings and body text, avoiding the harshness of pure black.
  - Secondary Text (`#68615A`): Warm taupe grey for subtitles, timestamps, and secondary captions.
  - Surface Stone (`#F5F2ED`): Warm parchment for cards, alternating sections, and chips.
  - Canvas Base (`#FCFBF9`): Warm alabaster white for the base screen background.
  - Hairline Rule (`#E8E3DC`): 1px borders and dividers.

## Typography

The typography couples the graceful serifs of **Playfair Display** with the clear, open rhythm of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display** commands titles, poetic bouquet names, collection headers, and editorial storytelling. It is styled with relaxed tracking and natural proportional figures.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clarity and warmth for descriptive details, floral composition specs, delivery timelines, pricing, and interactive controls.
- **Uppercase micro-labels** (`label-caps`) are used for category tags, seasonal notes, and badges with expansive letter spacing (`0.14em`), lending a boutique gallery aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around editorial cadence, pairing expansive white spaces with staggered, asymmetrical composition.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max-width container, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` side margins); 8 columns on tablet; 4 columns on mobile (`1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins).
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Stagger**: Showcase galleries leverage alternating column spans (e.g., span-7 featured hero arrangement next to span-5 detail vignette) to emulate modern magazine layouts.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` and above) between catalog groupings ensures arrangements are viewed as independent works of art rather than high-density inventory items.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, light-diffused tonality rather than aggressive shadows or dense borders:

- **Ambient Petal Drop**: Soft, low-contrast shadows tinted with warm umber and faint rose (`rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.04)` to `rgba(201, 76, 109, 0.06)`), with blur radii between 16px and 32px for floating navigation and hover states on cards.
- **Frosted Veil Surfaces**: Header bars, sticky WhatsApp floating bars, and filter sheets utilize semi-translucent backdrops (`rgba(252, 251, 249, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Hairline Restraint**: Structural separation relies on `1px` borders colored in `#E8E3DC`, creating crisp, featherlight divisions without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design system blends architectural structure with soft, organic curves:
- **Cards & Media Frames**: Defined with soft rounded corners (`rounded-lg`, 1rem) or gentle curves (`rounded`, 0.5rem) to reflect organic flower petals while preserving an editorial profile.
- **Chips, Badges & Action CTAs**: Employs full pill contours (`rounded-full`) for tactile intimacy, effortless tapping, and soft contrast against square photographic compositions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Bespoke Inquiry / WhatsApp Order)**: Pill-shaped, rich Deep Petal Rose (`#C94C6D`) or deep espresso (`#1E1B18`) background, white text in `label-lg`, with integrated subtle WhatsApp icon. No heavy dropshadows; gentle lift on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pill-shaped, transparent background with 1px `#E8E3DC` hairline border and `#1E1B18` text; transitions to faint rose mist (`#FDF2F4`) on hover.
- **Inline Link**: Understated Playfair Display or Plus Jakarta Sans with a trailing 1px underline offset by 4px.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Botanical Tags**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-md` horizontally.
- **Colorways**:
  - *Olive Tag*: `#F0F4EF` fill with `#5A7859` text for botanical composition tags (e.g., "Peonías Holandesas", "Eucalipto").
  - *Champagne Tag*: `#FAF6EC` fill with `#99752D` text for special occasions (e.g., "Edición Limitada", "Boda").
  - *Blush Tag*: `#FDF2F4` fill with `#C94C6D` text for signatures.

### Cards
- **Arrangement Showcase Card**: Warm stone base (`#F5F2ED` or pure `#FFFFFF`), 1rem border radius. Features high-aspect-ratio photography (3:4 or 4:5 vertical crop), understated title in Playfair Display, subtle pricing in Plus Jakarta Sans, and a discreet pill badge or tap action.
- **Hover Micro-interaction**: Delicate image scale (1.02x) with smooth bezier transition (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`), no abrupt color shifts.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls**: Crisp warm white background, 1px `#E8E3DC` border, gentle 0.5rem corner radius. Focus states illuminate with a subtle rose hairline border (`#C94C6D`) and zero ring shadow.
- **Dedication Note Input**: Editorial parchment style with custom line height and italic placeholder hinting at romantic calligraphy.

### Lists & Dividers
- Single-pixel horizontal dividers (`#E8E3DC`) paired with generous vertical spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`).
- Feature lists utilize miniature olive leaf or delicate diamond glyphs instead of standard bullets.

### Concierge WhatsApp Bar
- Sticky, bottom-docked or floating capsule pill optimized for one-thumb activation, allowing clients to send bouquet references, custom budget preferences, and delivery dates directly to the boutique florist in Oaxaca.
